SPEAKING-50
PART 1
Your partner asks you about yourself, your home, work or studies and other familiar topics.
EXAMPLE
Sunglasses
 Do you wear sunglasses?
 Why do you wear sunglasses?
 How often do you buy sunglasses?
 Have you ever lost you sunglasses?

PART 2
Describe a period in history that interests you
You should say:
What the period was
How you know about it
and explain why it is interesting

You will have to talk about
the topic for one to two
minutes. You have one
minute to think about what
you are going to say.
You can make some notes to
help you if you wish.

PART 3
Discussion topics:
History
Example questions:
Do you agree that learning history may help people understand the present?
Is it true that modern society has much to learn from the past?
What are the differences between learning history from books and from videos?
Why is it that some leaders fail to learn from the past?
How can people learn about history?
How can technology make learning history more interesting?
How do museums teach people history?
Are history museums useful?
